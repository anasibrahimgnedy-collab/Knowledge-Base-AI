## Overview
CS103 Digital Logic Design is a 3-credit-hour core course at academic level 1. It is offered in the Fall semester only. There are no prerequisites or other registration requirements.

## Description
Introduces Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential circuits, flip-flops and finite state machines as the basis of computer hardware.

## Topics
Boolean algebra; logic gates; Karnaugh maps; flip-flops; finite state machines.
