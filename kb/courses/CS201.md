## Overview
CS201 Data Structures is a 4-credit-hour core course at academic level 2. It is offered in every semester (Fall and Spring). Registration requirements - prerequisites: CS102.

## Description
Studies arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, heaps, hash tables and graphs together with their time and space complexity.

## Topics
Linked lists; stacks and queues; binary trees; heaps; hash tables; graph representations; Big-O analysis.
