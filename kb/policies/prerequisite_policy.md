## Prerequisites
A student may register for a course only if every prerequisite listed for that course has been passed. Prerequisites are cumulative: all listed prerequisites are required, not just one. A course that is currently in progress or was failed does not satisfy a prerequisite.

## Corequisites
A corequisite must be passed before, or registered in the same semester as, the course that requires it. If the corequisite course is not passed, the student must register for both courses together, and both must be individually eligible.

## Repeating passed courses
A student may not register again for a course that has already been passed. Only failed courses may be repeated.

## Additional requirements
Some courses require a minimum number of passed credit hours or a minimum cumulative GPA. These requirements are stated in the course overview and must be satisfied at the time of registration.
