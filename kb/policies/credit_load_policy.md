## Maximum credit hours per semester
The maximum registration load depends on the cumulative GPA of the student. A student with a cumulative GPA below 2.0 is on academic probation and may register for at most 12 credit hours. A student with a cumulative GPA from 2.0 up to but not including 3.0 may register for at most 18 credit hours. A student with a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher may register for at most 21 credit hours.

## First semester students
Students who have no cumulative GPA yet (new students in their first semester) may register for at most 18 credit hours.

## Minimum load
Full-time status requires at least 12 registered credit hours. A student whose eligible courses cannot reach 12 credit hours receives a warning from the advisor but is not blocked.

## Exceeding the limit
A registration that would exceed the maximum credit hours is rejected. Exceptions are not granted by the advising system; students must petition the dean in writing.
