## Core courses
Courses marked as category core are required for the Bachelor of Computer Science degree. Advisors should prioritise core courses at the student's own academic level before electives, so that the student stays on schedule for graduation.

## Electives
Elective courses allow students to specialise in areas such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, web and mobile development or distributed systems. Electives should be chosen according to the student's interests, goals and demonstrated skills, after core requirements at the student's level are covered.

## General education
General education courses such as Critical Thinking and Society complete the degree and may be taken at any time the level rule allows.

## Advising principle
Advisors may only recommend a course that passes every registration rule. Recommendations must cite the course overview and the relevant policy. A course that fails a rule must be reported with the rule it failed.
