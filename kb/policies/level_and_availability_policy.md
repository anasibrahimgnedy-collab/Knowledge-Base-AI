## Academic level restriction
Courses are numbered by academic level from 1 to 4. A student may register only for courses at or below the academic level recorded for the student by the registrar. A level 1 student may not register for level 2, 3 or 4 courses, and a level 2 student may not register for level 3 or 4 courses.

## Semester availability
Each course is offered in the Fall semester, the Spring semester, or both. A course can only be registered in a semester in which it is offered. Course overviews state the semesters in which the course is offered. A course offered only in Fall cannot be registered in the Spring semester.

## Minimum passed credit hours
Senior courses may require a minimum number of passed credit hours, stated in the course overview. The Capstone Project requires 60 passed credit hours. Professional Ethics and Law in Computing requires 30 passed credit hours.
